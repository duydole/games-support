Merge Drop – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Merge Drop ("the app") is developed by Do Le Duy. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Data we collect

None. The app does not collect, store on any server, sell or share any personal data. It contains no ads, no analytics and no third-party SDKs.

Data stored on your device

Your best score and your sound/haptics settings are saved locally on your device only. Deleting the app removes them.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Apple Game Center, your score is submitted to the app's Game Center leaderboard. This is handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy; we do not receive your Game Center account details.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
