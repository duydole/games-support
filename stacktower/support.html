FAQ

The leaderboard won't open.
Sign in to Game Center in Settings › Game Center, then try again.

There's no sound.
Check the sound toggle on the main menu and make sure your device isn't in silent mode.

Will I lose my best score if I delete the app?
Your local best score is removed, but scores already on the Game Center leaderboard stay there.
